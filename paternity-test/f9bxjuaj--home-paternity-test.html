Understanding Your Paternity Test Results

Interpreting paternity test results might feel daunting at first, but understanding the report is crucial for informed decision-making. Generally, the results will present either a conclusive match or exclusion. A result indicating a 99.9% probability of paternity confirms that the tested individual is the biological father. Conversely, a lower probability indicates that he is not the biological parent. Often, these reports include additional data, such as details about the genetic markers analyzed and the methodology used, which can provide further insights into the testing process. Should there be any ambiguity or further questions regarding the results, many providers offer consultation services to help clarify the findings. Consulting with a genetic expert can be particularly helpful for those who are navigating complex family situations or emotional circumstances. You can move forward with the necessary knowledge and prepare for any next steps that may be required.
